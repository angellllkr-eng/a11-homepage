Why A11

The system should make reality easier to inspect.

A11 combines frontier agent capability with practical controls for identity, tools, data, transactions, validation, and handoff. The goal is not to make every action automatic. The goal is to make the right action easier to understand and safer to authorise.

01

Clarity

Make system state, ownership, and next decisions visible to the person responsible.

02

Control

Keep authority bounded and distinguish preparation from consequential execution.

03

Continuity

Leave a record that lets the next operator inspect, verify, and continue the work.

Ownership and accountability

Ambition is welcome. Unsupported certainty is not.

Public surfaces should be premium, distinctive, and useful without presenting proposals, local checks, or intended integrations as completed external outcomes.

  • Repository validation is labelled repository validation.
  • Runtime state is labelled from direct observation.
  • Placeholders remain visibly placeholders.
  • High-consequence actions stay owner-controlled.
