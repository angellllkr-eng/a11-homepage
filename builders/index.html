Delivery discipline

Every implementation should leave a trail.

The trail is not paperwork added after the fact. It is the set of records that lets the owner understand what changed, what passed, and what still needs authority.

  • Typed contracts make service boundaries inspectable.
  • Idempotency makes safe retry behaviour explicit.
  • Validation commands create repeatable evidence.
  • Handoff notes preserve the next decision.
